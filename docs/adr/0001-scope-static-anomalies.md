# ADR 0001: Scope: static gravity anomalies

- Status: accepted (2026-10-22)
- Decision record: DEC-01 in MASTER_GUIDE §4.10

## Context

The data hold one epoch only; there are no temporal gravity changes or deformation (F-01, §3).

## Decision

Study FAA, SBA and CBA with their uncertainty and gridding. Temporal and deformation modules are out of scope.

## Change if

Multi-epoch gravity or geodetic data become available.
